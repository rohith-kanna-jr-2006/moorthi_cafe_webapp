The Morning Filter Kaapi Pour

Poured from an arm’s length into a dabarah tumbler to aerate the chicory blend, yielding the quintessential South Indian velvety golden froth.

menu_book

Cultural Trivia: The high pouring arc—locally termed 'Meter Coffee'—not only cools the boiling decoction naturally but preserves the volatile aromatics of the dark roast chicory seed without curdling fresh dairy.

Tawa to Plate: Golden Paper Roast

Milled from fermented heirloom parboiled rice and black gram, stretched razor-thin across sixty-year-old seasoned iron plates.

Culinary Legacy: The signature golden crunch relies on a generational wet-grinder technique that keeps batter aerated before meeting country ghee roasted at precise temperatures.

The Banana Leaf Bhojanam

A balanced sensory symphony honoring the Shadrasas—the six foundational Ayurvedic tastes—served upon freshly cut plantain fronds.

spa

Sacred Etiquette: Each element has an exact geometric spot on the leaf: salt and pickles on the narrow top left, payasam dessert on the bottom right, celebrating digestional harmony.

Steam from the Brass Steamer

Jasmine-white idlis pulled hot from damp woven muslin, boasting an ethereal, feather-light cloud crumb perfected through slow natural fermentation.

Craft Secret: Unlike modern non-stick moulds, wet muslin cloth allows dry steam to circulate freely without collecting moisture condensation on the cake crust.
