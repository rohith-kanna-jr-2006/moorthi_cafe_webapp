The Culinary Standard

Crafted with Uncompromised Integrity

We honor ancestral culinary science through four unwavering fundamentals that define every plate and cup served at New Moorthi Cafe.

grain

Traditional Stone-Grinding

Heavy granite stone grinders keep the batter naturally cool during rotation, retaining probiotic ferment cultures and delivering unparalleled fluffiness.

eco

Farm-Fresh Banana Leaf Dining

Pristine plantain leaves harvested daily enhance hot tiffins with subtle polyphenol aroma while upholding conscious, eco-positive South Indian hospitality.

water_drop

Pure Desi Ghee & Sesame Oil

Clarified dairy cow butter ghee and cold-pressed gingelly sesame oil provide rich authentic flavor and digestibility without refined oils.

verified

Zero Artificial Additives

No commercial food colors, chemical thickeners, or synthetic preservatives. Every aroma comes solely from whole spices roasted in-house.
